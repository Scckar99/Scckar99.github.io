# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Potential collaborators, peers, and visitors who want to understand Scckar's technical focus, projects, and writing from desktop or mobile devices.

## Product Purpose

Present Scckar's professional profile, project work, and articles clearly while building a credible and personal impression.

## Brand Personality

Clear, rational, and personal, with restrained technology and gaming character carried by existing content and assets.

## Anti-references

- Controls that visually compete with the site's content.
- Mobile navigation that feels like a compressed desktop menu, including right-aligned labels and isolated icons.
- Broad visual redesigns when a focused interaction fix is requested.

## Design Principles

- Keep content primary and navigation quiet.
- Make mobile interactions readable, predictable, and comfortable to touch.
- Preserve the existing identity while fixing concrete usability problems.
- Prefer focused improvements over unrelated redesign.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, touch targets of at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels, semantic labels, and reduced-motion alternatives for interface animation.
